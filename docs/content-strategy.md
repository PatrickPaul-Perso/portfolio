# Content Strategy

The portfolio should communicate within seconds that Patrick Paul builds reliable digital infrastructure, automates systems, integrates heterogeneous technologies, and prefers hands-on technical work.

## Primary audience

- Technical recruiters
- DevOps, SRE, and platform managers
- Organizations delivering operational platforms, R&D initiatives, proofs of concept, and technology demonstrations

## Content priorities

1. Practical outcomes
2. Technical depth
3. Clear constraints and decisions
4. Evidence of continued curiosity
5. Confidentiality-safe project descriptions

Avoid reproducing the résumé verbatim. Use project stories to show problems, approaches, technologies, and outcomes.
