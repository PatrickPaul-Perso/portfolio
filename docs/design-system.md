# Design System

## Direction

- Dark theme with layered blue and green accents
- High contrast and restrained visual effects
- Subtle references to networks, IoT, and additive manufacturing
- Content-first layouts with strong typography

## Tokens

- Background: `#07111f`
- Surface: `#0d1b2d`
- Primary accent: `#2ea8ff`
- Secondary accent: `#6ec6ff`
- Operational accent: `#61d696`
- Operational accent soft: `#9ce8bd`
- Text: `#e8eef6`
- Muted text: `#a8b8ca`

Motion must remain subtle and respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
