# Engineering Principles

- Build for maintainability.
- Automate repetitive work.
- Reduce operational complexity.
- Prefer open standards and portable solutions.
- Document important decisions.
- Design for failure and recovery.
- Keep humans accountable for consequential decisions.
- Solve real problems instead of chasing technology.
- Make systems understandable to the people who operate them.
- Prefer incremental improvement over disruptive rewrites.
- Choose the simplest architecture that satisfies current requirements.
- Leave the repository in a better state after every change.
