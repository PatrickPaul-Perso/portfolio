# Roadmap

The roadmap is intentionally incremental. Each milestone should leave the site functional, documented, and ready for review.

## Milestone 0 — Foundation

Status: complete.

- Astro and TypeScript foundation
- GitHub Pages deployment
- bilingual routing
- accessibility and responsive baseline
- generative AI transparency
- engineering and contribution documentation

## Milestone 1 — Landing page

Status: complete, pending human review.

- bilingual professional identity and calls to action
- professional value and motivation sections
- five-capability preview
- remote employment and consulting contact preview
- reserved professional-photo and contact areas
- infrastructure-inspired responsive visual system
- accessibility, routing, and responsive validation

## Milestone 2 — Capabilities

- design digital infrastructure
- connect existing systems
- automate operations
- observe and understand systems
- prototype new ideas

## Milestone 3 — Case studies

- EdgeX Foundry integration
- Linux, containers, and automation
- Raspberry Pi and microcontroller systems
- diagrams, constraints, approach, and outcomes

## Milestone 4 — Technical laboratory

- home automation and IoT
- 3D modelling and additive manufacturing
- open-source observability and data tooling
- selected experiments and prototypes

## Milestone 5 — Engineering journey

- selected career milestones
- publications and technical contributions
- engineering principles and working approach

## Milestone 6 — Contact and release readiness

- employment and mandate calls to action
- LinkedIn, GitHub, email, and résumé links
- SEO and social metadata
- final accessibility and performance review

## Version 1.0

The portfolio is ready to share with technical recruiters and hiring managers when all published claims, translations, links, media, and contact details have received human approval.
